- Added Gladia Enterprise API options to `GladiaSTTService` settings: `diarization` (the transcription frames' `user_id` becomes `"S0"`, `"S1"`...) with `diarization_config`, `numerals`, `emails`, `addresses` and `pii`; `EndOfTurnConfig.vad_threshold`; `speculation` as a `SpeculationConfig` with a threshold and entities; `PreProcessingConfig.speech_threshold="auto"`. With `enable_finalize=True`, the local VAD's end of speech sends Gladia's `finalize`. On the Enterprise API, `audio_enhancer` and `words_accurate_timestamps`, which it refuses, are left out with a warning instead of failing the session.
