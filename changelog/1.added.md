- Added end-of-turn detection to `GladiaSTTService` for the Gladia Enterprise API. Set `end_of_turn=EndOfTurnConfig(...)` in the settings: Gladia's speech start and end of turn then propose the user turn boundaries, the final that closes a turn is marked finalized, and the service recommends external user turn strategies. With `enable_eager_end_of_turn=True`, Gladia's `eager_end_of_turn` and `turn_resumed` drive `EagerUserTurnStrategies`. New events: `on_end_of_turn`, `on_eager_end_of_turn`, `on_turn_resumed` and `on_hold`.
